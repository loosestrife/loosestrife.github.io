Click the game to capture the mouse (Esc releases; dragging also turns the camera). The mouse turns the camera and WASD moves relative to it. Space jumps, and Space again while airborne against a wall wall-jumps. Nord: jump, then C or Shift to ground pound. Elf: hold click or F for the frost beam. Martian: click or F fires plasma. Press E next to the red car to drive (W/S throttle, A/D steer, E exits); you can run cops over, and only the embassy door works from the car. Test Valley also has a gather-and-craft loop: pick up the luna moth wing (southeast) and ice wraith teeth (northwest), press E at the Alchemy Station (southeast) to brew, then Q to drink: cops and zombies lose you for 25 seconds. Press E next to the Starfighter (north of the start) to fly a sortie: mouse or A/D steer, Space and Shift climb and dive, W boosts, S brakes, click or F fires lasers, R fires a homing missile, E twice leaves. Drink the invisibility potion (Q) and the Dreadnought at the end of the canyon can no longer see you: it only shoots at the last place it saw you shoot from, marked in red. Plug in content from the console, for example GSA.packs.races.mine = {...} then GSA.start({race:'mine'}). Engine events such as in:front, out:back, deliver and alarm_raised go through a global bus, so hook them from the console with GSA.bus.on('alarm_raised', (ctx) => ...) (the callback receives the live state plus ctx.heat(d)).
